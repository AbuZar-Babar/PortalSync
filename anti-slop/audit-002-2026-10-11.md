### Block 2: Purpose-Gate (Technique Justifications)

1. **R-01 (Color & Gradients)**:
   * *Technique:* Subtle radial indigo/cyan ambient backlight glows behind hero showcase and timeline.
   * *Purpose:* Provides architectural rim lighting that separates obsidian `#09090d` cards from the `#050507` deep canvas without color clutter.
2. **R-04 (Icons)**:
   * *Technique:* Contextual Lucide glyphs (`MousePointer`, `Eye`, `Wand2`, `Zap`, `FolderTree`).
   * *Purpose:* Visually represents the five discrete phases of browser automation (recording, inspection, healing, replay, filing).
3. **R-06 (Typography)**:
   * *Technique:* Monospace font on step badges (`01`, `02`) and system metrics.
   * *Purpose:* Establishes precision engineering feel while keeping primary headings in clean, highly legible sans-serif.
4. **R-07 (Background)**:
   * *Technique:* Pure dark obsidian `#050507`.
   * *Purpose:* Eliminates generic AI blueprint/dot grids; lets typography and UI mockups take center stage.
5. **R-08 (Button Arrows)**:
   * *Technique:* Small `ArrowUpRight` on primary CTA pills (`Start Free ↗`).
   * *Purpose:* Directional visual cue indicating navigation/modal trigger; omitted on secondary controls.
6. **R-10 (Glassmorphism)**:
   * *Technique:* `backdrop-blur-md` on floating header capsule and studio frame.
   * *Purpose:* Maintains text legibility as content scrolls beneath the fixed navigation; well within the 1-2 element dose cap.
7. **R-14 (Feature Cards)**:
   * *Technique:* Alternating left/right scroll timeline cards.
   * *Purpose:* Breaks layout monotony and paces information delivery down the page as the visitor scrolls.
8. **R-19 (Motion & Animations)**:
   * *Technique:*
     * Unconditional background looping mockup (Record -> Run -> Download).
     * Alternating scroll-reveal transitions on timeline cards (`transition-all duration-700 ease-out`).
     * Infinite horizontal marquee ticker (`animate-running-ticker`).
   * *Purpose:* Conveys autonomous product functionality without demanding manual user effort. Matches MOTION 2 dial.
